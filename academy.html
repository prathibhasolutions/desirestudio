DESIRE ACADEMY

Empowering Dreams, Shaping Creators

At Desire Academy, we believe that every passionate individual deserves the right platform to transform their creativity into a career. Founded by Dr. Soujanya, an internationally certified mentor with decades of experience in the fashion and beauty industry, our academy stands as a symbol of excellence, innovation, and empowerment.

We offer in-depth training in a range of creative and professional courses, including:

Fashion Designing Garment Construction Makeup Artistry Self-Grooming Styling Classes Mehendi Artistry

Whether you’re a beginner or an aspiring professional, our programs are designed to blend hands-on practice with creative theory and real-world experience. Our expert faculty, industry-oriented approach, and emphasis on individuality ensure that every student not only learns but grows with confidence.

At Desire Academy, students gain the opportunity to showcase their skills in live fashion shows, professional events, and earn international certifications that open doors worldwide.

Join us — where style meets skill, passion meets profession, and every talent finds its stage.
