WHO WE ARE

Our Story

Desire Studio began with a vision — to create a space where art, elegance, and individuality come together. Founded by Dr. Soujanya, an internationally certified mentor with a passion for empowering people, our journey started from a simple belief: every person deserves to look and feel their absolute best.

From a small dream to a thriving creative hub, we've grown into a studio that offers Couture, Makeup, Boutique, Academy, and Photography — all crafted with precision, love, and attention to detail. Every gown stitched, every brush stroke applied, every click captured, carries a part of our heart and your story.

Desire Studio is more than a place — it's a celebration of self-expression, confidence, and unforgettable moments. And as we continue to grow, one thing remains constant: your dreams will always be at the heart of everything we create.

Our Mission

"Empowering beauty, style, and confidence — one creation at a time."

We are dedicated to empowering students to transform their passion into profession, while providing our clients with exceptional services that celebrate their unique beauty and style. Through our comprehensive education programs and personalized services, we help individuals shine in their personal and professional lives.
