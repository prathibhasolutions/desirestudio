MEHENDI ARTISTRY

Celebrate Moments with Elegant Mehendi

At Desire Studio, we offer professional Mehendi services tailored for every occasion—weddings, festivals, or private celebrations. Our skilled artists bring creativity, precision, and passion into every design.

Let your hands and feet tell a story! From classic bridal artistry to trendy fusion patterns, every design is personalized for you and your loved ones.

OUR SERVICES INCLUDE

Bridal Mehendi

Traditional, full-hand to elbow & foot designs customized for your wedding day.

Engagement/Pre-wedding Mehendi

Beautiful designs for engagements, sangeet, and pre-wedding events.

Festival Mehendi

Karwa Chauth, Teej, Rakhi, Eid & more—celebrate every festival in style!

Guest & Group Mehendi

Fun, elegant designs for friends, families, and group events.

Kids Mehendi

Simple, safe, and joyful patterns for little hands—skin-safe guaranteed.

Custom Designs

Personalized names, portraits, or themed modern styles—just share your idea!
